1. THREE PURPOSE-BUILT CV FORMATS
==================================================

Keep these three formats, but make them genuinely different:

1. PROFESSIONAL CV
Detailed professional document, normally 2–3 pages.

Recommended structure:
- Header/contact
- Professional Profile
- Core Expertise
- Professional Experience
- Selected Projects / Systems
- Leadership & Entrepreneurship
- Education
- Technical Skills
- Languages
- Training / Certifications / Achievements when verified
- Selected professional links

2. COMPACT RESUME
Maximum 1–2 pages.
Prioritize:
- Header
- Short profile
- Core expertise
- Most relevant experience
- 2–3 selected projects
- Education
- Skills
- Languages

Remove long descriptions and unnecessary links.

3. EXECUTIVE / PROFILE CV
Leadership-oriented profile.
Prioritize:
- Executive identity
- Executive profile
- Leadership
- Entrepreneurship
- Major engagements
- Selected impact/work
- Capabilities
- Education
- Languages
- Essential professional links

Do NOT simply reuse identical content/order for all three formats.
